Workflow

From receipt to insight in six steps

Every stage is designed to minimise manual effort while keeping humans in control of financial decisions.

01

Capture the Expense

Upload a receipt image, import a spreadsheet, paste a bank statement, or enter details via a simple form. Multiple capture paths, one structured record.

02

Extract Transaction Details

OCR and document AI pull out vendor, date, amount, currency, and description. The result is clean, machine-readable data ready for the next stage.

03

AI Categorisation

The system recommends the best expense category using description, vendor, and context. Users can accept or correct — corrections improve future suggestions.

04

Validate & Save

Business rules check required fields, amounts, and duplicates. Once confirmed, the transaction is stored with full audit metadata.

05

Calculate & Analyse

Totals, category breakdowns, and budget comparisons are computed automatically and shown on the dashboard in real time.

06

Generate Reports & Insights

Ask natural questions or schedule reports. AI surfaces spending patterns, missing receipts, and items needing review.

Development Path

Four clear stages to a production-ready system

1

Reliable Core System

Authentication, expense CRUD, categories, search, dashboard totals, monthly reports, and solid PostgreSQL foundation. No AI yet — just dependable expense management.

2

AI-Powered Processing

Receipt uploads, OCR extraction, AI category recommendations, confidence indicators, user confirmation flows, and duplicate warnings.

3

Workflow Automation

Inbound receipt channels, review notifications, scheduled reports, budget alerts, and exports to accounting tools.

4

Real-User Validation

Pilot with a small set of businesses. Measure time saved, extraction accuracy, categorisation accuracy, and correction frequency. Iterate on real data.
